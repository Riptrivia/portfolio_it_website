Why graphics?

AGE 10 · 2013

The benchmark that started it.

When I was about 10 years old, my uncle and I picked up the parts at Fry’s Electronics and built my first PC around a GTX 450 for one clear mission: play Crysis. That build turned graphics settings, frame rates, and hardware limits into something I could explore firsthand.

That same curiosity drives this workbench today—understand the machine, push the pixels, and see what becomes possible.

How it works

One page.
Two render paths.

The 3D scenes use a full-screen WebGL fragment shader and switch procedural scene functions on the GPU. The retro set uses Canvas 2D and deliberately low internal resolutions to recreate the character of older home-computer effects. Quality controls change render scale; animation pauses automatically when the tab is hidden.
