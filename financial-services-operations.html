What I handle.

Operational responsibilities translated into clear outcomes without exposing company-specific systems, users, tickets, or procedures.

01 / ENDPOINT REFRESH

Windows 11 migration

Support refresh planning and execution across laptops, desktops, and virtual endpoints. Prepare devices, confirm data readiness, configure cloud-file access, validate applications, track assets, and complete user handoff with minimal disruption.

02 / INCIDENT RESPONSE

Walk-up and remote L2 support

Triage hardware, software, account-access, application, and connectivity incidents. Reproduce issues, isolate causes, perform approved remediation, communicate status clearly, and preserve useful case history in ServiceNow.

03 / ENTERPRISE MOBILE

Phone lifecycle management

Provision and enroll corporate phones, configure approved email and applications, assist with MFA and compliance, prepare replacements, validate functionality, and complete secure user handoff.

04 / ASSET CONTROL

Hardware and inventory coordination

Track issued equipment, replacements, refresh devices, and loaners while coordinating setup, validation, return status, and documentation across the endpoint lifecycle.

05 / DOCUMENTATION

Reliable, reusable support notes

Structure call details, troubleshooting steps, actions, and outcomes into consistent notes so the next technician can understand the case quickly and users receive more dependable follow-through.

06 / WORKFLOW DESIGN

Automation and visual operations tools

Build sanitized local templates, browser utilities, spreadsheet automations, and timeline dashboards to reduce repetitive work and make operational information easier to scan and act on.
